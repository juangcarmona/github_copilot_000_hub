# Why Planning Becomes Critical in Agentic Workflows

- `Agents act autonomously` Without a plan, they choose scope, architecture, and boundaries on their own
- `Speed amplifies direction errors` An agent that builds fast in the wrong direction creates more rework than a slow human
- `Plans are the control surface` The plan is how you steer an agent - not mid-stream corrections, not post-hoc reviews
- `Ambiguity is the enemy` Anything left unspecified becomes an assumption the agent makes for you
- `Validation requires intent` You can only verify output if you wrote down what "correct" looks like before building
