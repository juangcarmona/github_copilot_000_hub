# Common Failure Patterns

<div class="grid grid-cols-5 gap-3 mt-6 text-xs">

<div class="rounded-xl border border-red-200 p-3 bg-red-50">
<div class="font-bold text-sm mb-2">Tool-First Rollout</div>
<p>Licenses before standards.</p>
<p class="mt-2 font-semibold">→ Fragmented usage, policy gaps.</p>
</div>

<div class="rounded-xl border border-orange-200 p-3 bg-orange-50">
<div class="font-bold text-sm mb-2">No Baselines</div>
<p>Dashboards without pre-AI data.</p>
<p class="mt-2 font-semibold">→ Impact unmeasurable.</p>
</div>

<div class="rounded-xl border border-yellow-200 p-3 bg-yellow-50">
<div class="font-bold text-sm mb-2">Productivity Theater</div>
<p>More PRs, more code, more tickets.</p>
<p class="mt-2 font-semibold">→ Quality degrades silently.</p>
</div>

<div class="rounded-xl border border-purple-200 p-3 bg-purple-50">
<div class="font-bold text-sm mb-2">Governance After Incident</div>
<p>No policy until something breaks.</p>
<p class="mt-2 font-semibold">→ Reactive controls disrupt teams.</p>
</div>

<div class="rounded-xl border border-blue-200 p-3 bg-blue-50">
<div class="font-bold text-sm mb-2">One-Size Adoption</div>
<p>Same workflow for all teams and phases.</p>
<p class="mt-2 font-semibold">→ High-risk phases under-governed.</p>
</div>

</div>

<div class="mt-6 text-sm text-center opacity-80">

These are not edge cases. They are the default failure modes when organizations scale access faster than operating discipline.

</div>

<div class="mt-3 p-3 rounded-lg bg-blue-50 border border-blue-200 text-sm text-center">

💬 **Which of these patterns do we recognize in our organization?**

</div>
