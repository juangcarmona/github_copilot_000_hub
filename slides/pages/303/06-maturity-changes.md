# What Changes At Each Maturity Level

<div class="text-xs mt-4">

| | People | Process | Technology | Governance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1 · No AI** | Manual work | Traditional SDLC | No AI tooling | N/A |
| **2 · Exploratory** | Individual experimentation | Unchanged workflows | IDE-level AI | None — shadow AI risk |
| **3 · Structured** | Shared patterns | Instruction-shaped workflows | Repo-level agents, skills | Team conventions |
| **4 · Scaled** | Cross-team adoption | Standardized AI workflows | Platform-level enablement | Baselines, visibility, budgets |
| **5 · Governed** | AI-native engineering culture | Spec-driven, validation-first | Integrated AI across SDLC | Policy, audit, continuous optimization |

</div>
