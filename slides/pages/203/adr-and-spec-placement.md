## Architectural Intent

::left::

## Why ADRs and Specs Matter

AI (and new contributors) cannot follow decisions or requirements that are never written down. ADRs record what was decided, why, and alternatives considered. Specs describe expected behavior and acceptance criteria.

::right::

## Placement and Discoverability

```
README.md
ARCHITECTURE.md
docs/
└── adr/
docs/
└── specs/
```

Keep ADRs and specs near the project docs and reference them from `README.md` or `ARCHITECTURE.md` so they are easy to find.

**Core message:** AI cannot follow decisions or requirements that are never written down.
