# Three Modes of Interaction

::left::

## Autocomplete

Inline suggestions as you type — responds to what you're writing in real time.

Best for: routine code, boilerplate, small completions, fast iteration.

## Chat

Interactive conversation with AI in a panel or inline.
Context-aware reasoning and code generation.

Best for: exploration, understanding, targeted generation, debugging.

::right::

## Agent

Autonomous multi-step execution across your codebase.
Plans, implements, and iterates without continuous prompting.

Best for: complex tasks, multi-file changes, task-driven workflows.

<div class="mt-6 text-sm opacity-70">

Progressively autonomous from left to right. Start with the simplest mode that fits the task.

</div>
