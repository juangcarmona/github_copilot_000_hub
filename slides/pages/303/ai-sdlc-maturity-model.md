# AI SDLC Maturity Model

<div class="grid grid-cols-5 gap-3 mt-6 text-xs">

<div class="rounded-xl border border-gray-200 p-3 bg-gray-50">
<div class="font-bold text-sm mb-1">1 - No AI</div>
<p class="mb-2">Manual SDLC. No AI in the workflow.</p>
<p class="text-red-600 font-semibold">Risk: none from AI, but competitive gap widens.</p>
<p class="mt-2 opacity-70">Next: pilot with a safe team and low-risk tasks.</p>
</div>

<div class="rounded-xl border border-orange-200 p-3 bg-orange-50">
<div class="font-bold text-sm mb-1">2 - Exploratory</div>
<p class="mb-2">Chat, autocomplete, ad hoc agents. Output quality depends on personal habits.</p>
<p class="text-red-600 font-semibold">Risk: non-repeatable results, shadow AI usage.</p>
<p class="mt-2 opacity-70">Next: share patterns, add repo instructions.</p>
</div>

<div class="rounded-xl border border-yellow-200 p-3 bg-yellow-50">
<div class="font-bold text-sm mb-1">3 - Structured</div>
<p class="mb-2">Instruction files, reusable prompts, agent profiles, review expectations.</p>
<p class="text-red-600 font-semibold">Risk: team-level. Works for early adopters, not the whole org.</p>
<p class="mt-2 opacity-70">Next: baselines, dashboards, cross-team standards.</p>
</div>

<div class="rounded-xl border border-blue-200 p-3 bg-blue-50">
<div class="font-bold text-sm mb-1">4 - Scaled</div>
<p class="mb-2">Usage visibility, baselines, model strategy, consistent controls across teams.</p>
<p class="text-red-600 font-semibold">Risk: inconsistent enforcement, slow feedback loops.</p>
<p class="mt-2 opacity-70">Next: policy, budget gates, quality metrics, audit trail.</p>
</div>

<div class="rounded-xl border border-purple-200 p-3 bg-purple-50">
<div class="font-bold text-sm mb-1">5 - Governed</div>
<p class="mb-2">AI is embedded in the SDLC with policy, observability, quality gates, and continuous improvement.</p>
<p class="text-red-600 font-semibold">Risk: complacency. Policy must evolve with tooling.</p>
<p class="mt-2 opacity-70">Next: optimize costs, improve standards, iterate.</p>
</div>

</div>

<div class="mt-5 text-sm text-center opacity-70">

This model is not "how much AI you use." It is how safely and effectively AI is integrated into the SDLC.

</div>
