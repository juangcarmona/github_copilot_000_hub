# Governance vs. Enablement

<div class="grid grid-cols-2 gap-8 mt-6">
<div>

## Governance Without Enablement

❌ Policies block adoption  
❌ Developers bypass approved tools  
❌ Shadow AI usage increases  
❌ No feedback loop to improve  
❌ "We tried AI, it didn't work"  

</div>
<div>

## Enablement Without Governance

❌ Costs spike unpredictably  
❌ Sensitive data leaks to external tools  
❌ No audit trail for AI decisions  
❌ Teams diverge in practices  
❌ "We can't afford this anymore"  

</div>
</div>

<div class="mt-8 p-4 rounded-lg bg-green-50 border border-green-200 text-center">

**The operating principle:** Govern what must be consistent. Enable everything else.

Centralize: data policy, budget caps, approved tools, model defaults.  
Decentralize: instruction files, workflow design, agent configuration, model overrides.

</div>
